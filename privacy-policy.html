InterviewIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. Introduction

InterviewIQ is an application designed to help users organize, analyze, and practice interview questions.

2. Information We Collect

When you sign in using LinkedIn, InterviewIQ may receive basic profile information that is authorized by you through LinkedIn's authentication process.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to authenticate users and provide the InterviewIQ service.

4. LinkedIn

InterviewIQ uses LinkedIn authentication. We only request the permissions required for the functionality provided by the application.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application.

7. Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the InterviewIQ project owner.
